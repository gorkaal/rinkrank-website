Club rankings methodology and coverage

Each team receives a normalized divisional percentile of (division size − League Rank) ÷ (division size − 1) × 100. A club's score is the unweighted average of its eligible teams' percentiles. Divisions with fewer than two teams do not contribute to that average. Ties are broken by Club Win % and then lower average Power Rank. All averages use existing verified team-level values; no power ratings are recalculated.

Win % = (wins + 0.5 × ties) ÷ games × 100. Zero-game records have unavailable win percentages, never zero. A club with missing approved teams or missing division data is provisional; its numeric position is not a fully comparable league ranking. GP, W, L, T, GF, GA and points are summed across included teams.

League Rank refers to published GTHL division position; Power Rank and Momentum are independent PuckLadder analytics. Average ranks are descriptive, not the club ranking formula.
