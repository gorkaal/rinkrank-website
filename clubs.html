Club rankings methodology and coverage

Each team receives a normalized divisional percentile of (division size − League Rank) ÷ (division size − 1) × 100. A club's score is the unweighted average of its eligible teams' percentiles. Divisions with fewer than two teams do not contribute to that average. Ties are broken by Club Win % and then lower average Power Rank. Category filters include only teams in the selected A, AA and/or AAA categories. A club's average considers its eligible teams equally, regardless of how many teams the club fields in a category. All averages use existing verified team-level values; no power ratings are recalculated.

Win % = (wins + 0.5 × ties) ÷ games × 100. Zero-game records have unavailable win percentages, never zero. Club rankings are generated solely from PuckLadder’s verified division datasets. Every included team is assigned to one club. Clubs absent from the verified data are excluded, and operating fewer teams or age groups does not lower coverage. GP, W, L, T, GF, GA and points are summed across included teams.

League Rank refers to published GTHL division position; Power Rank and Momentum are independent PuckLadder analytics. Average ranks are descriptive, not the club ranking formula.
